##Project Overview
This project is an interactive Coffee Sales Analysis Dashboard developed in Microsoft Excel to analyze coffee shop sales performance. The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into meaningful business insights using PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers, and Excel formulas.
It enables users to monitor sales trends, product performance, customer purchasing patterns, and location-wise revenue through an intuitive and interactive interface.

##Objective
The primary objective of this project is to:
Analyze coffee shop sales data to identify business trends.
Track key performance indicators (KPIs) such as Total Revenue, Total Transactions, and Total Quantity Sold.
Compare product performance across different categories and product types.
Understand customer purchasing behavior based on product size and time of purchase.
Evaluate sales performance across different store locations.
Build an interactive dashboard that supports data-driven business decisions.

##Conclusion
The dashboard indicates that coffee and bakery products are the primary revenue drivers, while sales are concentrated during the morning hours. Since Regular and Large-sized beverages account for the majority of sales, maintaining adequate inventory for these sizes can help prevent stock shortages. Location-wise sales are relatively balanced, suggesting that consistent product availability and service quality should be maintained across all stores.

##Business Recommendations:
The analysis shows that coffee products generate the highest sales, making them the key revenue contributors. Sales are strongest during the morning hours, indicating peak customer demand early in the day. Regular and Large-sized beverages represent the largest share of sales, reflecting customer preferences. Additionally, sales are distributed relatively evenly across all three store locations, suggesting consistent performance across the business.
